⚽ FUTSAL

Rules & Format

  • 01

    5v5 + 2 rolling substitutes (max 7 roster). At least 4 players to start.

  • 02

    Match: 2 × 8 minutes, 3 min half-time. Continuous clock (ref may stop for injury).

  • 03

    Group stage → 8-team knockout. Points: Win 3, Draw 1, Loss 0.

  • 04

    5 group winners + 3 best runners-up qualify. Tie-break: GD, GF, GA, H2H.

  • 05

    Knockout draw → extra time → 5 penalties → sudden death.

  • 06

    Yellow = 1-minute suspension (team plays short). Red = sent off.

  • 07

    No offside. Fair play required. Referee decisions are final.

🏸 BADMINTON

Rules & Format

  • 01

    Knock-out from the first round (no league). Only registered players.

  • 02

    Till Quarter-Finals: single set to 15. Golden point at 14–14 (next rally wins).

  • 03

    Semi-Finals & Final: best of 3 to 21, win by 2, cap 30 (at 29–29 next point wins).

  • 04

    Non-marking shoes compulsory. Report 15 minutes before match.

  • 05

    Walkover after 10 minutes no-show. Umpire's decision is final.
